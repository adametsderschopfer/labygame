Project adaptations: imported FBX as an Unreal skeletal mesh; rebuilt materials
from supplied texture maps, converted normal-map Y convention for Unreal,
configured translucent visors, and scaled/oriented the character for laby.
Further adaptations: retargeted Unreal template locomotion, procedural crouch,
and separate first-person hood/head masking. Epic template animations are
separate engine content and are not covered by the Hazmat model's CC BY license.
Original source files are retained under ArtSource/Characters/HazmatSuit3/Original.
